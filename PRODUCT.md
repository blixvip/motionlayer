# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Desktop-first web application with a responsive review surface.

## Stack

TypeScript, Next.js, PostgreSQL/Prisma, Redis/BullMQ, S3-compatible storage, isolated Node render workers, FFmpeg, Chromium, and pinned HyperFrames Producer/Core 0.8.19.

## Users

Creators finishing educational, software, business, tutorial, talking-head, and social video who need professional, explainable motion graphics without manual keyframing.

## Product Purpose

MotionLayer accepts a script, script plus narration, or locked edited video. It preserves the source, builds an open-vocabulary semantic knowledge graph, proposes a sparse motion plan, resolves licensed assets, and authors editable HyperFrames graphics. Success means the user can inspect why each visual exists, approve only the useful beats, revise one item surgically, and export a composited MP4 or transparent professional overlay.

## Positioning

MotionLayer is an intelligent motion-design finishing layer, not a raw-footage editor or generic AI video generator. It clarifies, identifies, compares, proves, and emphasizes while protecting the completed edit.

## Operating Context

Projects progress from estimated script timing to narration-aligned timing to final-video timing without discarding approved semantic, asset, and design decisions. A locked source is never trimmed, reordered, retimed, or overwritten.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Every meaningful span is interpreted in document context; the planner deliberately chooses no graphic when imagery would not help.
- Material decisions carry evidence, confidence, warnings, provenance, and a safe fallback.
- Low-confidence identities and factual conflicts require review; unknown-license assets remain preview-only.
- Approved beats compile to deterministic, local-asset HyperFrames compositions and render in isolated Producer workers.
- A local/fake provider path keeps development and tests useful without paid APIs.
- Unsupported post-MVP controls are labeled rather than simulated.

## Brand Commitments

Working name: MotionLayer. Script Studio and Graphics Studio use a calm indigo-charcoal editing suite with pale actions and compact, readable controls. Authored motion frames draw their palette from a real reference, pairing condensed display type with a colored serif-italic accent, tactile texture, asymmetric depth, and original or licensed local assets. The existing locked-video editor and Creator Style Library retain their established registration and source-evidence behavior; source footage remains protected.

## Evidence on Hand

The master build specification is product authority. Official HyperFrames documentation, the installed package types, the verified fixture renders, and stored manifests are technical authorities. No customer claims, licenses, facts, or third-party identities may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Protect the original edit.
- Suggest first, generate second.
- Motion must have a reason.
- Make uncertainty and provenance visible.
- Keep approvals reversible and local revisions surgical.
- Prefer fewer purposeful graphics over constant decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, preserve keyboard navigation and visible focus, label icon-only controls, honor reduced-motion preferences, and never require color alone to communicate state.
