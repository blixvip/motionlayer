# Style Director quality bar

## Intent

MotionLayer should turn one script into a directed editorial video, not a sequence of interchangeable AI title cards. The source remains dominant; direction is compact, persistent, and auditable through the render receipt.

## Required evidence

- Desktop and mobile composer captures show an unclipped source-first layout and all Style Director controls.
- A rendered frame from each profile proves a different material and spatial grammar.
- HyperFrames lint, runtime, layout, and contrast checks pass on a real MP4 render.
- Unit tests prove density, motion energy, and creative-note modes affect generated output.

## Profile targets

- **Editorial proof:** hard black-and-paper hierarchy, decisive evidence bars, lime used only as a registration signal.
- **Field notes:** warm stock, irregular local red-pencil texture, lighter-weight type, asymmetrical documentary composition.
- **Blueprint:** cool construction grid from a local SVG, measured frames, technical labels, outlined rather than filled comparisons.

## Motion targets

- Restrained or quiet direction uses shorter travel, longer easing, and thinner progress notation.
- Expressive or kinetic direction uses larger travel, quicker arrivals, and stronger progress notation.
- Each entrance resolves into stillness; no ambient drift, bounce-heavy motion, or arbitrary choreography.

## Rejection cues

- The three profiles are the same layout with different colors.
- Semantic role names such as THESIS, PROOF, or TAKEAWAY appear as visible kickers.
- A detached accent repeats a word or number already visible in the source sentence.
- Creative notes appear only in metadata and do not affect planning or motion.
- Gradients, glow, glass, generic card grids, fake telemetry, or decorative UI chrome appear.
